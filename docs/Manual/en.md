# ReservaYa user guide

[Versión en español](es.md) · [Open ReservaYa](https://djbfmoum5t7ct.cloudfront.net/)

Use **Idioma / Language** on the public portal to change languages. To open a dashboard, select **Sign in** and use your owner, professional, or customer account. If you belong to more than one business, check which one is selected in the top bar.

## Book as a guest

1. Open the business’s public page and select **Book now**. Under **Service**, select the service you want and choose **Next**.
2. Under **Schedule**, choose a professional if prompted and select an available date and time. Check the time before going on.
3. Under **Details**, enter your name and phone number. You can also enter your email address. Review the booking summary and select **I authorize the processing of my personal data** to continue.
4. Under **Payment**, check the amount, choose one of the options shown, and select **Pay**. Follow the payment provider’s instructions.
5. When you return, check the appointment status and save the reference if shown. If payment is still pending, check its status before starting another booking.

If **Service** is empty and **Next** is disabled, ask the business to check its service catalog and availability. If a time is no longer available, go back to **Schedule** and choose another.

## Manage the business as an owner

1. In the business dashboard, open **Services**. Select **New service**, enter the name, duration, and price, and select **Create service**. Check that the switch in the service row is on if you want to offer it on the portal.
2. Under **Professionals**, you can invite the person who will provide the service and choose their assigned services. To review or change a professional’s weekly hours, select **Schedule** on their card.
3. Under **Appointments**, check bookings and their statuses. Under **Reports**, choose a preset period or fill in **From** and **To** and select **Apply** to view the financial summary for that period.

## Check your schedule as a professional

1. In your dashboard, open **My schedule** and select a day.
2. Check the time, service, and status of your assigned appointments. If the schedule is empty, check the selected day and business.
3. If you need to cancel an appointment and **Cancel** is available, read the summary and estimated amount before confirming. Check the resulting status in your schedule.

## Review your appointments as a customer

1. In your dashboard, open **Upcoming appointments** for active bookings. Use **History** to see previous bookings and their statuses.
2. Check the business, service, date, time, and payment status.
3. To cancel, select **Cancel** if available and review the policy and estimated amount. After confirming, read the system message and check the appointment under **History**.
